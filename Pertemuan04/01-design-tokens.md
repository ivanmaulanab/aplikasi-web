# 01 - Design Tokens Sistem SIPINJAM FT UNY
Mata Kuliah: Praktik Aplikasi Web (INF60295) - Pertemuan 4

## 1. Skala Warna (Color Palette)
- `color-primary`: `#0284c7` (Aksi utama, tombol submit, navbar)
- `color-primary-hover`: `#0369a1` (State hover tombol utama)
- `color-primary-light`: `#e0f2fe` (Badge aksen dan background penanda)
- `color-surface`: `#ffffff` (Latar kartu dan form container)
- `color-background`: `#f8fafc` (Latar kanvas dasar antarmuka)
- `color-border`: `#e2e8f0` (Garis pembatas komponen dan field)
- `color-text-main`: `#0f172a` (Judul, heading, dan teks utama)
- `color-text-muted`: `#64748b` (Keterangan kapasitas, fasilitas, dan meta)

## 2. Tipografi (Typography)
- Font Family: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`
- Headings:
  - H1 (Judul Halaman): `1.5rem` (24px), font-weight: `800`
  - H2 (Subjudul Section): `1.125rem` (18px), font-weight: `700`
  - Body Text: `1rem` (16px), font-weight: `400`
  - Label / Meta: `0.75rem` - `0.875rem` (12px - 14px), font-weight: `600`

## 3. Spacing & Radius
- Skala Spacing: `4px (0.25rem)`, `8px (0.5rem)`, `16px (1rem)`, `24px (1.5rem)`, `32px (2rem)`
- Radius:
  - Input field dan button: `8px (0.5rem)`
  - Kartu kontainer: `12px (0.75rem)`
  - Status pill badge: `9999px (full)`
